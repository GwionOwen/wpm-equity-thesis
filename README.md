# WPM Equity Thesis

Equity research thesis on Wheaton Precious Metals, analysing relative performance vs gold, valuation, real yields and key risks.

## Overview

This project presents my investment thesis on Wheaton Precious Metals (NYSE: WPM) as a capital-light way to express a bullish view on gold.

Wheaton is a precious-metals streaming company. It provides financing to mining operators in exchange for the right to purchase a portion of future production at fixed low prices. This model offers exposure to rising gold prices with lower operating risk than many traditional miners.

## Key Result

Since the 24 October close:

- WPM returned +26.6%, rising from 98.15 to 124.22
- Gold rose about +10%, from roughly 4,120 to 4,530

## Investment Thesis

My core view was that WPM offered a stronger risk-reward profile than holding gold directly because:

- the streaming model provides capital-light exposure to rising gold prices
- fixed purchase terms support strong margins and cash generation
- the business avoids many operational risks faced by miners
- the macro backdrop remained supportive as real yields softened and gold demand stayed strong

## Macro View

A key part of the thesis was the behaviour of US real yields.

- US 10-year TIPS real yields were around 1.9%
- the main risk was that real yields would remain choppy and range-bound rather than clearly trend lower
- lower or softer real yields improve the relative appeal of gold and support the valuation of streaming companies such as WPM

## Why I Trimmed

After the move, I reduced the position because:

- much of the upside had already been pulled forward
- the valuation premium left less margin for error
- real yields were not yet clearly trending lower

## Why WPM Still Stood Out

Even after trimming, I continued to prefer WPM to gold itself because:

- it offered capital-light exposure through the streaming model
- fixed purchase terms reduced operating risk and protected margins
- it provided a more stable earnings profile than many traditional miners
